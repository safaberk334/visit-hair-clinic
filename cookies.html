Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies are used on the Visit Hair Clinic website (visithairclinic.com) and how you can manage them.

1. What Is a Cookie?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. They are used to keep the site working correctly, remember your preferences and improve your experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie TypePurpose
Essential CookiesRequired for the site's core functions (e.g. remembering your cookie consent choice).
Preference CookiesRemember your choices, such as your language selection.
Analytics CookiesHelp us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

3. How Can You Manage Cookies?

You can accept, refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers accept cookies by default, but you can change this at any time. If you disable essential cookies, some parts of the site may not work correctly.

You can also state your preference through the cookie notice shown on your first visit.

4. More Information

For detailed information about how your personal data is processed, please see our Privacy Policy and KVKK Disclosure.
