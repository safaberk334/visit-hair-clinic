FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Your Questions Answered

We've answered the most common questions about hair transplantation for you.

Is a hair transplant painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won't feel pain during the operation. Any mild tenderness afterwards is easily managed with prescribed painkillers.

How long does it take and how many grafts are needed?

The operation usually takes 6-8 hours and is completed in a single session. The number of grafts depends on the degree of hair loss and your target density; during a free consultation we create a clear plan based on your photos.

What is the difference between FUE, DHI and Sapphire FUE?

All three are modern, stitch-free techniques. In FUE grafts are extracted one by one; Sapphire FUE uses sapphire blades for faster healing; DHI implants grafts directly with a special pen for a dense, natural hairline. Our specialist selects the best method for you.

Are the transplanted hairs permanent?

Yes. The transplanted hairs are taken from a genetically loss-resistant area, so they are permanent and grow for a lifetime. The 'shock loss' in the first weeks is normal; these hairs regrow permanently within 3-4 months.

What is the recovery like and when can I return to work?

Most patients return to daily life within 2-3 days. Protecting the recipient area is important for the first 10 days. Scabbing clears in about 10 days, and the permanent, natural result fully appears within 12 months.

How does the process work for international patients?

For our international guests we plan everything end to end: airport transfer, accommodation and the operation. Send your photos via WhatsApp and we'll build a personalized treatment and travel plan together.
